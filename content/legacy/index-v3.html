Grown-up guide & school resources

Practice 3–5 sounds at a time. Tap Listen, say the sound together, then try “Hear it 3 times.” Picture names are visual memory cues; every audio button plays a sound or consonant cluster, never a whole word or letter name. Stars mean practiced, not assessed.

Short phonics clips are supplied by Phonicademy. The clips have been trimmed, volume-matched, and peak-limited for clearer playback. Blend practice joins separate phoneme clips; it is not a new recording of a person saying each connected blend. No browser speech synthesis or word-reading fallback is used. Pronunciation may vary by accent. This is an original companion activity, not the school’s official cards or videos. Ask your teacher for the school articulation videos if you need an exact accent match.

Progress saves in this browser. The library includes core sounds, digraph spellings, beginning and ending two-sound blends, and advanced clusters. Three letters can represent two sounds (thr = /th/ + /r/). Three-plus-sound cards are an optional challenge. This is not a verified copy of the school’s card inventory.
